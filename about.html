I'm Sanskar, a software developer building software, developer tools, and digital products in the open.

My interests span a broad range of software. I work with systems languages such as Rust, C, and C++; build for Android with Kotlin and Java; develop for the web with JavaScript, TypeScript, and React; and also use C#, .NET, and Python. Beyond applications, I'm interested in game development and in AI and machine learning.

I publish code on GitHub, take part in the developer community, and create digital products. This website brings all of it together in one place.
