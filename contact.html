Before you write

  • Business inquiries: a short description of what you have in mind, plus any timelines or links that help.
  • Support requests: what you were trying to do, what happened instead, and the steps to reproduce it. For problems with this website, include your device and browser.
  • Never send secrets: please don't include passwords, API keys, access tokens, or other credentials in an email.
