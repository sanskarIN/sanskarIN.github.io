Publishing research

Share original technical research, experiments, surveys, or reproducible investigations. The website's publication review is not academic peer review.

  1. Define the question

    Provide a clear title and abstract, background, research question and scope.

  2. Document the evidence

    Explain methods, sources, experimental conditions and results. Distinguish observations from interpretations and include limitations.

  3. Attach supporting material

    Use PDF, PPTX, DOCX, Markdown or HTML files for reports and reproducibility notes, and BibTeX (.bib) files for citations and bibliographies. Files are public; remove private information and respect licenses. Each file must be smaller than 10 MB, with up to five per post.

  4. Submit and revise

    Select Publish in Research in the account editor, or include the research tag in the GitHub issue form. Valid research submissions automatically create a pull request for maintainer review before publication. Choose the article language, optional subtitle, publication details (type, category, version and counts) and custom URL slug before publication; approved edits retain the original article URL.
