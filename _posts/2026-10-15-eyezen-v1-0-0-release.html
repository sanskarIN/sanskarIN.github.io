EyeZen v1.0.0 — Your Screen. Your Comfort. Your Environment.

EyeZen v1.0.0 is now available.

It's the first release of my local-first desktop application for workspace personalization, built with Rust, Tauri 2, React and TypeScript.

The release includes ten application interface themes, nine starter workspace profiles, weekday and overnight scheduling rules, optional break reminders, and accessibility options.

Settings are kept locally, with import, export and backup support. The project is open source under the Mozilla Public License 2.0.

The app is designed for Windows, macOS and Linux, with unsigned CI-built installers attached to the release.

Device-specific testing is still needed. EyeZen does not yet control real monitor brightness or operating-system color temperature; its comfort settings are stored preferences rather than physical display adjustments.

This release establishes the foundation for more capable workspace routines and future platform integrations.

Feedback and contributions are welcome.
