## FrameTrellis v1.0.0 Is Here

<strong>A new milestone for local-first, open-source video editing.</strong>

FrameTrellis v1.0.0 is the first stable release of my desktop video editor, built with C++20, Qt 6/QML, MLT, and FFmpeg.

The goal is to provide practical multi-track editing tools while keeping your media and projects on your own computer.

No account is required. There is no cloud processing, telemetry, or advertising.

### Edit, preview, and export

FrameTrellis includes a multi-track timeline for video and audio, clip trimming and splitting, snapping, real-time preview, audio waveforms, transitions, and clip-level picture and sound adjustments.

You can export projects to MP4 using H.264 video and AAC audio. Project files use a validated format, with atomic saving, undo/redo, automatic saving, crash recovery, and missing-media relinking.

### Download FrameTrellis

The first stable release includes two Windows x64 options:

- <strong>Installer:</strong> Install FrameTrellis on Windows 10 (version 1809 or later) or Windows 11.

- <strong>Portable ZIP:</strong> Extract the archive and run the application.

Linux users can build the application from source. Linux packages and macOS support are not available in v1.0.0.

### What comes next?

Titles, subtitles, keyframes, proxy media, additional export formats, and expanded platform support remain on the roadmap.

I'm continuing development with an emphasis on a dependable editing workflow, clear documentation, and feedback from people who use the software.
