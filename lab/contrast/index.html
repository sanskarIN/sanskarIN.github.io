Reading the results

WCAG 2.2 requires at least 4.5:1 contrast for normal text at AA and 7:1 at AAA. Large text needs 3:1 at AA and 4.5:1 at AAA. Essential non-text interface graphics typically need 3:1 at AA against adjacent colors.

Large text means at least 18 pt regular (about 24 CSS px) or 14 pt bold (about 18.7 CSS px). These results assess only opaque foreground/background colors, not font thickness, transparency, images, gradients, hover states, or full WCAG conformance. The suggested foreground is obtained by blending toward black or white and may be unavailable at 7:1 on some backgrounds. Pass/fail uses the unrounded ratio.

Reference: WCAG 2.2 Contrast (Minimum), Contrast (Enhanced), and Non-text Contrast. Use Copy CSS variables to reuse the current colors in a stylesheet. All calculations remain on this page and no input is uploaded or saved.
