Limit: 32,000 characters and 300 lines per side. The comparison runs entirely in your browser.

Paste text on either side, then select Compare.

Line changes

+ added · − removed · space unchanged. Line order is preserved.

    How the comparison works

    Compares lines using a longest-common-subsequence algorithm. A changed line may appear as one removal and one addition. The result is text-only, and nothing is uploaded.
